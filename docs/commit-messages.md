# Commit Messages

Before creating or amending a commit:

- Use a capitalized imperative subject of 50 characters or fewer, with no
  trailing period.
- Separate an optional body from the subject with a blank line.
- Wrap body lines at 72 characters and explain what changed and why, not how.
- Put issue references at the bottom of the body.

Source: <https://cbea.ms/git-commit/>
